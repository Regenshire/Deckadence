Insert symbol

Presets fit a standard modern card. Adjust position and box size for other layouts. Click the card with Text selected to move the block. Type o2, og, ot or a sequence like o2ogog for colored ProxyGlyph symbols. Codes such as {W/U}, {T} and {ARTIST} also work.
