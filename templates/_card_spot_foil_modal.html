1 · Apply automatic spot foiling

Reduces tiny dots and holes from artwork texture. Applies when you click Apply, before text and border overrides. Your later brush edits keep their detail.

100% applies full foil; 50% applies half the amount inside the foil areas. Paint, Magic Wand, and Match Color use this percentage. Erase uses it to remove foil. This does not change how much area is selected.

0% means no border override. Other values are a percentage of card width. Text and border choices override the automatic result when you click Apply. AI can miss decorative letters; use the editing tools for touch-ups.

2 · Remove small pockets

Clean the current mask without regenerating it. Remove isolated foil dots, fill small clear holes surrounded by foil, or do both.

Choose a preset or enter any whole number from 1 to 65,536. This counts total connected pixels in this mask: 25 pixels could be a 5 × 5 pocket. Larger sizes remove more detail. Areas connected to the card edge stay intact. Filled holes inherit the strongest foil amount along their edge.

Preview the result, then Save for this card. Undo restores the previous mask.
