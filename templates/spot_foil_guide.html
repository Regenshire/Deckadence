Start here / One card at a time

Choose. Apply. Refine.

Effect 1 and Effect 2 add printable artwork through independent masks. Choose Foil Mask for foil or Gloss for a clear coating. Automatic selection, touch-up tools, and cleanup edit the active layer. Each keeps its own selection and Undo history; Save stores all four layers. Gloss effect, strength, and overlay color are under Preview finish in Gloss mode. Gloss previews a raised clear surface with edge highlights, shadows, and reflections that follow the card tilt. It stacks above Rainbow and Surge foil (including saved legacy Chocobo foil), keeping their patterns visible underneath.

On an Effect tab, choose a style under Printable effect. Pattern size changes the detail size; Density changes the amount of detail. New pattern changes its arrangement. Stars, halftone dots, and neon glow have their own color picker. Fracture, gold, and silver fill the selection with solid material at 100% strength. Effect 2 sits above Effect 1, with gloss above both. These textures print as colored artwork; reflective foil and raised gloss still require the corresponding stock or finishing process.

Flame Foil 1 creates red-orange ember flames; Flame Foil 2 creates blue fire. Enable Stacked flames to cover the background. Confetti Foil, Water Beads, and Frost Foil each mix four distinct motifs. Pattern size adjusts their size, Density adjusts their number, and New pattern reshuffles them. Water Beads leave the artwork visible through their clear centers.

Fracture’s Translucence reveals the artwork’s shading through the foil while keeping its colors. Effect strength controls the overall opacity. Both settings apply to previews and exports.

Baked artwork. Saving automatically caches a new image of the original artwork with the Edit layer, then Effect 1 and Effect 2 applied. Card views, zoom previews and native Copy Image use this finished image. The original artwork and all editable layers remain intact. Foil and Gloss stay separate above the artwork. Changing artwork or effects rebuilds the cache; older saved cards bake when first viewed. Print and image exports always include saved artwork edits, with their chosen Effect 1 and Effect 2 switches.

The editor opens on Effect 1. Every printable effect has an Effect rotation slider; the selection mask stays in place. Dark Mirror Shards has eight glass templates, a Shard size slider (25–200%), and a tint color. Shard size scales the layout around the card’s center without changing the mask or alternate image. With Mirror off, it is a standalone glass effect. Turn Mirror on, select a theme, and generate one alternate scene that appears inside the shards. AI uses the current edited artwork inside your configured art box and excludes detected frame and text. Maintain art style analyzes its rendering before compiling a model-specific mirror recipe. The shared planner then extends the themed illustration to the card edges and optional margin, preserving completed artwork between stages. Progress identifies analysis, scene generation and extension. DEV copies include Export AI diagnostics for inspecting each stage. FLUX, SDXL, Stable Diffusion 1.5 and SD-Turbo support this workflow. InstructPix2Pix remains available for other image editing but cannot create these full-canvas scenes. Compact models may produce less coherent extensions; review the result before saving. Your mask determines where the finished shards appear. Review the generated artwork before saving. The alternate image has independent rotation, zoom, horizontal skew, and horizontal/vertical position controls. Position is measured as a percentage of the canvas; positive values move right or down. At 100% zoom it fills the canvas without stretching; lower zoom reveals more of the image and may reveal space around it. Changing the template, mask or these controls reuses the saved image; changing the theme requires a new render.

Outpainting margin adds background around the scene and asks AI to keep the main subject centered. New scenes default to 30% on each edge: 30% of the width on the left and right, and 30% of the height above and below. FLUX generates a continuous wider view from the completed artwork; it can adjust details rather than preserve the center pixel for pixel. The extra background stays outside the normal view until you move, rotate or zoom the alternate image. Choose Custom under Theme to enter your own art direction. Maintain art style asks AI to match the source artwork’s medium, brushwork, texture and shading while applying either a preset or custom theme. It is on for new scenes; uncheck it to explore a different style. This is prompt guidance, not a guarantee. Generate again after changing the theme, prompt, style setting or margin. Existing saved scenes stay unchanged until regenerated.

View generated art, next to Generate / Regenerate, opens the complete saved or newly generated image before shard clipping, including its outer background. Use Fit to see the whole image or 100% to scroll through its full detail. It also works with local AI disabled. Regenerate requests a fresh variation; failed or cancelled attempts keep the previous scene.

Use Copy Mask, switch to another layer, and choose Paste Mask to replace that layer’s selection, or Append Paste to merge the copied mask with it. Append Paste keeps the stronger coverage where selections overlap, preserving soft edges without increasing opacity on repeated pastes. Both options also accept shapes copied with Copy Path. Effect settings stay unchanged, and Undo restores the previous selection. The copied mask is available until you close the editor.

For Oil Slick, choose Silver & Black Art in Effect 1, then copy its mask into Effect 2 and choose Oil Slick Rainbow. Raise Black depth to deepen shadows, adjust Silver brightness for darker metal or brighter highlights, and use Contrast for the overall tonal separation. The second layer’s strength adjusts the rainbow without lifting the black shadows. Both layers remain independently editable and can be included or excluded in Print / Export.

Step-and-Compleat · Phyrexian Symbols, Mana Symbol Foil, Dragonscale Foil and Gilded Filigree have copyable paths, size, rotation, position, transparency and color controls. Copy Path transfers the shapes at full coverage to Foil Mask or Gloss. Halo Foil adds irregular flowing rings; Ripple Foil adds finer curved bands. Neon Ink colors the selected artwork while retaining its detail. These are printable visual treatments; reflective foil and raised gloss come from the separate finish layers.

The compact Printable effect controls separate Shard layout, Image inside shards and Generate artwork. Shard horizontal and vertical position move the glass layout independently of the alternate image, after applying size and rotation. Positive percentages move right or down. Copy Path copies the transformed shard shapes into the same mask clipboard, including the gaps and without artwork, tint or the current selection. Use Paste Mask on Effect 1, Effect 2, Foil Mask or Gloss to use those shapes as a selection. Copying does not change the current mask or generate new art.

Chocobo Track is a printable effect in Effect 1 and Effect 2, using the original track shapes. Adjust Pattern size, Effect rotation, Track tint and strength. Horizontal and Vertical position move the tracks along the card axes, after rotation. Transparency runs from 0% (full effect) to 100% (invisible), on top of the strength setting. Copy Path copies the complete track shapes at the current size, rotation and position, independent of transparency, strength, tint and the painted mask. Paste Mask replaces a selection; Append Paste merges with it. Use either on Foil Mask or Gloss to finish the same tracks. Print / Export’s Effect 1 and Effect 2 toggles control the printable tracks. Previously saved Chocobo foil cards retain their legacy appearance.

Generative Art AI is an optional package installation under Card Finishing Studio in Plugin Manager. Choose a complete package and a processor: NVIDIA CUDA with native BF16 support (RTX 30-series or newer, or equivalent), supported AMD Radeon, or CPU. Setup tests the selected processor with the precision used by generation and artwork analysis. Package sizes include the image models and shared artwork analyzer; processor libraries require additional space. Component readiness and download progress are shown during setup. CPU rendering is slower. Images stay on your PC, and saved scenes can be viewed and printed with generation disabled. The Effect 1 and Effect 2 switches in Print / Export control these layers.

Masks and settings stay editable. Saving also caches a PNG overlay for card views. PDF and image exports render from the saved masks and settings at output resolution.

  1. Open the card’s spot foiling icon.The card appears on the left with a live Rainbow or Surge foil preview. Use Maximize for more room; Restore returns to the smaller window. New cards start without a saved mask.
  2. Choose what to find.Use Bright areas, Strong colors, Edges and texture, Dark areas, or Everything. Subject and Background use local AI. Choose None to skip automatic selection and keep the current mask for manual editing. Choose Whole card for full-art layouts; Fixed art box uses the outlined rectangle.
  3. Choose Foiled or Opaque.Foiled reveals foil in matching areas. Opaque · foil the rest keeps them opaque and reveals foil elsewhere in the region. To make bright areas opaque, pair that option with Bright areas. Text and border treatment can override the result.
  4. Set Area selected and Foil strength.Bright areas at 35% targets the brightest 35% of all pixels, including the background. A card with a large black background may have most of its colorful artwork selected. Increase Area selected to include darker pixels. At 100%, every pixel is selected before text, frame, and border treatments. Foil strength controls coverage within those shapes: 50% gives half coverage. Equal colors and cleanup can change the final area.
  5. Choose text and border treatment, then Apply.These choices override the automatic selection. Follow automatic selection leaves it unchanged. For full-art lettering, use AI detected and check Preview text. Apply spot foiling uses all the settings above the button.
  6. Exclude dark or light colors if needed.Open Clean up → Exclude colors, choose Darker than or equal to patch or Lighter than or equal to patch, then set the patch color and click Apply exclusions. This removes foil from matching brightness values in the current mask, including text and borders. It is separate from automatic selection. Undo restores it. For a white underbase, no foil means retaining white ink.
  7. Touch up, then clean up.Paint adds foil; Erase removes it. Magic Wand selects connected colors; Match Color finds similar colors across the card. Open Clean up to invert foil, remove small dots, or adjust edges. Undo and Redo are beside the preview tabs. With Pan in Preview, hover to tilt or drag to move a zoomed card.
  8. Review coverage.Mask overlay shows foil in the color chosen under Preview finish; fainter color means lower coverage. All Overlays shows all four masks together in their chosen colors, with overlapping colors blended. Tools still edit only the active layer. Use Mask only in the individual overlay view to hide the artwork and see unselected areas in black. These preview choices do not change the saved mask. Tool amount mirrors Foil strength. A 50% brush stroke adds half coverage; separate strokes build it up. For Foil Mask and Gloss, effect strength changes the screen preview. For Effect 1 and Effect 2, it changes the printed artwork too. Check partial coverage with a print proof.
  9. Save when it looks right.Save keeps the draft. Use Cancel to leave without saving; unsaved artwork edits require confirmation. Escape stays in the window while editing artwork. To restore the regular finish, choose Remove spot foiling, then Save.

Enabling Isolation snapshots its existing shared masks; turning it off returns to the shared masks.

Plugin Manager stores starting preferences. Changing them does not automatically foil cards or overwrite existing selections. A saved mask is tied to the printing and face; review its alignment after changing alternate artwork.

Edit v1 / Artwork changes

Paint, repair and add text.

Open Edit to work on a transparent modification layer above the original image and below Effect 1 and Effect 2. The right-hand groups contain Paint & color, Text and Generative edit; you can keep several groups open. Paint has its own active color, compact saved swatches and Pick from art. Fill covers the selection; Recolor retains shading. Use the + next to the swatches to save a color, or Edit colors to remove one. Erase removes painted edits; Delete text removes an added text block. Undo and Redo are separate from the finish masks.

The compact Select toolbar above the canvas stays available: Rectangle, Ellipse, freehand Lasso, Selection Brush, Magic Wand and Color Match. Magic Wand selects connected matching colors; Color Match selects matching colors across the card. Selection Brush selects an area without painting color. Choose New, Add or Subtract, or hold Shift to add and Alt to subtract with any Edit selection tool. The selection limits painting, fill and AI edits and stays in place while you use other controls. In Paint, Shift still locks a stroke horizontally and Ctrl locks it vertically. Deselect restores unrestricted painting. Edit selections are temporary and separate from finish masks.

Quick controls provides additional brush controls beside the canvas; the full controls remain in their original right-hand groups. Use its arrow button to collapse or expand the dock. On wider windows, drag either pane divider to resize the workspace. Sizes and the collapsed state are remembered in this browser. Focus a divider and use arrow keys to resize, or double-click it to restore its default size. On smaller screens, the inspector moves below the canvas.

The main layer tabs stay in the right panel on every tab. The preview toolbar stays on the left: Preview shows edits with all finishes, All Overlays shows the finish masks, Edit layer hides finishes, and Original shows the untouched source. Original is read-only: edit controls and Undo are paused while changes are hidden. Use Back to editing to restore the previous preview, or Pick color from original to sample the source. Pan is available on every tab; zoom in to move around the card. In Edit, hold Space over the canvas to pan temporarily, or use H for Pan. B selects Brush, E Erase, and [ / ] change brush size. The circular cursor shows the brush footprint. Keyboard shortcuts do not run while typing in text or numeric fields.

Soften selection edges is optional. Choose a feather radius and apply it to the current selection before painting or generating an AI repair. It blends the boundary into nearby artwork. Undo restores the previous selection, and the original image is never changed by selecting or feathering.

In Text, choose New and a text-block preset for title, mana cost, type, rules, flavor, artist, legal text or power/toughness, then Add text block. Presets provide standard modern-card positions, box sizes, fonts and styles. Title and type use Beleren Proxy, rules use Plantin MT Pro, legal and artist credit use Beleren Small Caps, and symbols use ProxyGlyph. Click an existing box or choose it from Text on this card to edit it. Clicking elsewhere does not create or move text. Drag Move text to reposition, or drag an edge or corner to resize. Resizing changes wrapping while keeping font size; optional Shrink text to fit changes that behavior. Text has its own color. Position & formatting contains alignment, spacing, bold, italic and exact coordinates.

Use Symbols or right-click the text field to open the searchable symbol picker. It inserts at the cursor without changing the block's font. Shortcuts include o2, og, ot and sequences such as o2ogog. Colored mana pips get their proper background colors. Braced codes such as {R}, {W/U} and {ARTIST} also work. In prose, use {R} and {X} for standalone red and variable mana so ordinary words “or” and “ox” stay text. Artist credit uses ProxyGlyph's lowercase a through {ARTIST}.

Text presets use larger card-readable sizes, with compact line spacing and a separate paragraph gap for rules text. Existing text retains its stored size and position; Reset block defaults applies the new preset. The percentage fields are rounded for readability; typing or formatting preserves the box’s exact placement. Undo and Redo restore the affected text box and keep it selected. Black, white and ivory text shortcuts help match different frames. A warning identifies text that exceeds its box; enlarge the box, reduce the size, or turn on Shrink text to fit. The fit indicator shows the effective size when shrinking is needed. Focus a move or resize handle and use arrow keys for one-pixel adjustments, or Shift+arrows for ten pixels.

To repair existing lettering, select it and use Text → Remove text & preview. Text detection identifies the lettering and repairs those strokes from nearby background instead of replacing the entire selected rectangle. Inspect the result carefully on textured or detailed backgrounds. For other changes, open Generative edit, choose a downloaded model, and use Generate preview, Remove object, or Extend artwork. The job shows its actual stage, elapsed time and Cancel generation. A status banner remains visible while using other groups. The completed result opens an enlarged view of the selected area labeled AI preview · not applied. Compare Original and AI result; Fit card shows the whole card. Apply result keeps it in the Edit layer, while Discard leaves the artwork unchanged. Similar results are flagged as little visible change rather than assumed successful. Failed jobs show a retry path. AI quality still depends on the model and selection.

Artwork style has three choices. Match artwork follows the selected model’s recipe. Recipes that need a written description use the included local analyzer to describe the edited artwork; repeated analysis is reused only for identical artwork and analysis instructions. FLUX Extend artwork matches the reference directly, using its adjacent colors, brightness and texture. It also checks for color drift and adjusts the generated extension when the retained artwork provides a reliable comparison. Custom description uses your own description instead of analysis or color matching. No style guidance omits both. Edit generation includes painted edits already on the card. Editable text is kept separately. Extend artwork excludes detected frame and text from its reference, and protects the clean central artwork. Inspect the preview for seams or unwanted subjects before applying it.

Preparation & variation lets you inspect the reference and final prompt before generating. Preparation performs detection, optional analysis, and prompt compilation; it does not generate an image or lock the next variation. Leave Seed blank for a new random variation on every attempt. Enter a number or choose Use this seed again to repeat a variation with the same inputs and settings. Generation details shows the model, recipe, reference images, observations, prompt, parameters and warnings, and can export the generation plan. A seed alone cannot reproduce an edit after changing its reference, recipe or model.

The AI Prompt Dictionary in Studio Settings contains the analyzer instruction and separate Edit recipes for each model and operation. These recipes control prompt wording, supported conditioning, token limits and generation settings. Long user instructions are rejected when they cannot fit a model; they are never silently truncated. Experimental recipes require visual review, and an unsupported operation is rejected. Other generative features continue using the dictionary’s existing model entries until they are connected to the planner.

Save in the dictionary editor writes only local overrides. Reset a recipe, the analyzer instruction, or all entries, then Save to apply the reset. Export for distribution exports the complete validated dictionary. To ship it, replace plugin_src/card-finishing-studio/generation_prompts.json in the source distribution with that export. The local ai_prompt_dictionary.json remains a user override file. The last 100 prepared plans are retained locally under the generation runtime’s generation-plans folder, with input hashes and settings rather than additional image copies.

Generation packages: Studio Settings installs complete packages: High End - FLUX.2, Mid Tier - SDXL, Lightweight - SD-Turbo, and Classic Editing - SD 1.5 / InstructPix2Pix. Every package includes its image models, the shared artwork analyzer, frame/text detection and processor runtime. FLUX also includes its outpainting adapter. Already downloaded components are reused, and settings show component readiness plus the current download’s progress. Install without switching adds a package while retaining your default. Edit lists models only from complete packages on the enabled processor; unsupported operations stay disabled. Saved artwork and manual tools remain available without generation. Hosted image providers are planned separately and are not available yet.

Setup progress survives an app restart. If setup is interrupted, choose Resume & enable package or Resume without switching; completed downloads are reused. Verify files checks the processor and generation libraries, the selected package’s model files and stored download checksums, shared frame/subject weights, and the local text detector, with progress and cancellation. It does not download models or change your default. If verification finds damaged files or libraries, resume setup to repair them. Healthy model downloads are kept. Library updates wait for an active generation to finish. Shared assets such as the analyzer serve multiple packages.

The shared artwork analyzer runs before the image model. When GPU memory is limited, it can keep some weights in system RAM; the progress message identifies this slower mode. Available system RAM is still required, including for Lightweight packages. Close other memory-heavy applications if analysis cannot reserve enough space.

The SDXL package includes separate models for its workflows. Edit uses the dedicated SDXL inpainting checkpoint to condition generation on the surrounding artwork and selection. Mirror uses SDXL base. Selecting SDXL in Edit chooses the appropriate checkpoint automatically; both components must finish installing before the package is ready.

Before a new NVIDIA runtime download, setup checks the installed driver’s GPU information when available. A known unsupported card stops setup before downloading CUDA libraries. The installed runtime then runs a real processor test before downloading image models.

SDXL Extend artwork also measures colors beside the selection and uses them in its style description. The recipe’s palette_source chooses reference for measured colors or analysis for the analyzer’s color description. This affects Match artwork only. Generation details includes the measured RGB samples and coverage, while retaining the analyzer’s original observations for comparison.

Touch up / Precise mask editing

Straight strokes and marquee boundaries.

While painting or erasing, hold Shift to lock the stroke horizontally, or Ctrl to lock it vertically. The lock starts at the current brush position; release the key to paint freely. If both keys are held, Shift takes priority.

Choose Marquee (M), then set Marquee shape to Rectangle or Round / oval and drag on the card. Hold Shift while drawing an oval for a perfect circle. Paint, Erase, color selection, fill, clear and invert operate only inside that boundary, including its curved edge. Delete clears inside; Esc or Deselect marquee removes the boundary without changing the mask. It stays aligned when zooming and carries across Effect 1, Effect 2, Foil Mask and Gloss. The boundary itself is temporary and is never saved or printed.

Copy Mask copies only the area inside the marquee. Draw a destination marquee and use Paste Mask to fit the copy into it, preserving everything outside. Append Paste merges the selections. Without a destination marquee, a cropped copy returns to its original position. With no marquee when copying or pasting, the existing whole-mask workflow is unchanged. Undo restores edits.

Ellipse is a direct shortcut for an oval boundary. Lasso draws a freehand boundary, and Selection Brush builds a boundary along your brush stroke. These tools are available both above the canvas and in Touch up. Drawing a boundary does not paint the mask; Paint, Erase, Clear Mask and other mask operations then work inside it.

Copy Path is also available for Galaxy Foil, Confetti Foil, Glitter 1, 2 and 3, Halftone Dots, Big Stars and Water Beads. It copies their current pattern geometry, including size, density, variation and rotation, independently of strength, tint and the painted mask. Galaxy copies the discs and small stars without the sky wash; Water Beads includes the clear centers of the droplets. Paste into Foil Mask or Gloss to finish those same shapes.

Dark Mirror Shards uses antialiased edges for the glass, bevels and clipped alternate artwork. Saved scenes do not need to be generated again; previews, baked artwork and exports use the smoother rendering.

03 / Hand off the right file

A PDF layer is not an ink channel.

A named spot separation carries print intent. A PDF visibility layer organizes objects. An alpha channel carries transparency. The RIP—the software that prepares printer passes—must interpret the chosen format correctly.

  1. Save the masks on your cards.Only saved masks in the active image library are exported. Cards without a saved mask get no spot ink. For white ink across the whole card, open the card editor, choose Touch up → Clear selection, then Save for this card. An empty saved foil selection keeps the white plate; Remove spot foiling removes it.
  2. Open Print / Export → Settings.Enable Include saved spot-foiling ink layers · PDF and choose the Print workflow for this job. It applies to all cards; cards only store their foil masks. Choose Artwork + named spot layers for Roland. Ink and layer details lets you adjust the ink name and purpose for this export. Resolution, display color, and margins come from that workflow’s Plugin Manager preferences.
  3. Choose percentage or solid coverage.Preserve tool percentages keeps soft edges and partial coverage. For white underbase, 100% foil becomes 0% white, and 0% foil becomes 100% white before the ink coverage limit is applied. Solid areas only uses a 50% ink cutoff. Foil transfer, commercial foil, and toner always use solid masks.
  4. Choose printable effects.Include Effect 1 and Include Effect 2 independently turn the saved artwork layers on or off for this job. PDF and image exports bake the enabled effects into the artwork. Foil, Gloss and White Inverse remain separate finishing plates. These switches do not change the card design.
  5. Include gloss when needed.Enable Gloss layer exports saved gloss as the named RDG_GLOSS ink. Leave it off to omit gloss. This works independently of the foil checkbox and requires Artwork + named spot layers. Gloss coverage uses its saved selection directly. The Gloss layer sits above artwork, below the optional White Inverse Layer.
  6. Choose the optional white overlay.Under Ink and layer details, Include White inverse Layer adds white wherever the exported spot mask is clear. Partial coverage is reversed too: 25% spot becomes 75% white. This is white artwork for color blocking, not another named printer ink. Top to bottom, the layers are White Inverse Layer → Art Layer → Spot Foil Layer. Without it, artwork sits above the spot layer.
  7. Check the PDF in the RIP.Named spot export contains true ink separations plus visibility layers for artwork and each ink. The spot plate uses your workflow’s display color (magenta by default); it is not the rainbow screen effect. Hide artwork and the optional white overlay in a layer-capable viewer to inspect the spot mask. Check the ink channel in the RIP and print a small proof on your actual stock.
  8. For a separate mask pass.Choose Black-only ink mask, then export the artwork with spot ink and decorative Foil Overlay off. Keep identical page, card, bleed, back-face, and registration settings. Use 100% print scale for both. Each export uses the chosen workflow’s ink channel.

The PDF keeps artwork and finishing separate. The original card pixels are retained; decorative screen foil is omitted during spot export. Masks follow card rotation, back-face placement, bleed, and rounded-corner clipping. Labels and registration marks remain in the artwork layer. New masks use the artwork’s available detail, up to 3024 × 4224 pixels. The editor shows the actual dimensions. Increasing export PPI alone cannot restore detail in an older saved mask.

04 / Tune the mask

What each setting controls

Selection defaults start new card edits. Cards save their foil masks. Print / Export chooses the workflow, ink purpose, spot name, PDF format, and coverage for the whole job. Workflow preferences supply resolution, display color, margins, and overprint behavior. The printer’s RIP controls ink passes, physical print order, curing, and media profiles.

Layer purpose
White underbase hides foil; foil / adhesive applies foil; varnish coats the selection. The custom workflow lets you choose among all three.
Spot channel name & overprint
Use the RIP’s exact spot swatch name. Artwork sits above the spot layer with overprint to retain the separate ink plate. PDF layer order does not determine the printer’s physical ink order.
Spot display color
In Plugin Manager → Card Finishing Studio → Configure workflows, choose a color or enter a six-digit hex value such as 838588 or #838588. Each workflow remembers its own color. Save workflows and export a new PDF; existing card masks do not need to be reapplied. This changes the spot swatch’s display color, not its ink name or coverage. Keep RDG_WHITE for Roland white ink. Workflows using the same ink name in one PDF must use the same display color.
Binary or grayscale
Binary uses an on/off region. Grayscale permits intermediate coverage. Transfer, commercial foil, and toner presets require a solid 100% mask.
Maximum coverage
Caps the intended ink / material channel value. It is not a calibrated foil-strength control or a replacement for the RIP’s ink limits.
Expand / shrink ink area
Positive millimeters expand deposition; negative values shrink it. Shrinking white can reduce white fringes. Check small details and registration with a sample.
Inset from card trim
Excludes deposited material near the cut edge. For white-underbase work, that can leave reflective stock exposed at the edge. Set it for the selected process.
Mask resolution
300, 600, or 1200 PPI sets the exported coverage mask resolution at final size. Automatic selection analyzes the available source detail, up to 3024 × 4224 pixels, without first shrinking it to a thumbnail. More pixels cannot recover details missing from the source. It is not the printer’s hardware DPI.
Automatic selection cleanup
Balanced smooths small edge irregularities and removes tiny disconnected dots and holes. Stronger produces broader, cleaner areas and can remove fine lettering or texture. Off preserves every selected detail. Cleanup runs only when you click Apply, before text and border overrides; it does not change later brush edits during PDF export.
Remove small pockets
Open Clean up to remove foil dots, fill clear holes, or do both on the current mask. Choose a preset or Custom for any whole number from 1 to 65,536 pixels. A 5 × 5 pocket contains 25 pixels; the same count covers less area on a higher-resolution mask. Click Apply pocket cleanup, review, then Save. Undo restores the previous mask. Diagonal pixels count as connected; pockets touching the card edge stay intact. Filled holes inherit the strongest foil coverage along their edge. Larger limits can remove small lettering and decorative details.
Initial selection & region
Choose Bright areas, Strong colors, Edges and texture, Dark areas, or Everything. Region limits the selection to the Standard art box or includes the Whole card. Set how much area to select in 1% steps, then Apply on a card.
Text & border treatment
Follow automatic selection, keep these areas opaque, or foil them. Use Whole card and AI detected text for full-art cards; Standard art box uses an approximate frame rectangle.
Text outline & smoothing
Select contrasting, light, or dark letters, or select whole text blocks. A positive outline widens that selection; a negative outline thins it. Smoothing softens its edges. Preview text shows the selection in cyan without switching your preview tab. Use Apply text only to update lettering while keeping the rest of the mask, or Apply spot foiling to rebuild the complete mask. Pixel widths scale with the card image, like the brush size. Ornate lettering may need touch-ups.
Color swatches & Sample
Touch up shows colors drawn from the card artwork. Choose a swatch or use Sample (I) to pick a color without painting. Apply color across card adds or removes foil wherever that color matches. Tolerance includes nearby shades; Tool amount controls coverage. The card remembers your last eight picked colors. Magic Wand (W) remains a connected selection; Match Color (C) selects matching pixels throughout the card when you click.
Shape and edges
Grow or shrink the current mask, smooth edges, or use Sharpen edges to tighten soft boundaries. Click Apply edge changes; Undo restores the previous mask. Sharpening preserves flat areas of partial coverage. Under Wand and Match Color, Selection edges adjusts only the next color selection.
Brush shape and softness
Choose Round or Square for Paint and Erase. Brush size sets the diameter or square width. Softness feathers the edges. Tool amount sets the maximum strength of one stroke; separate strokes can build up coverage. A hard brush keeps a crisp edge.
Invert foil
Under Clean up, Invert foil swaps foil and opaque areas across the current mask, including painted edits. Partial coverage is reversed too: 25% becomes 75%. It does not use Tool amount. Undo restores the original mask.
Review & reset
Review the card preview, then Save. Reset this workflow restores only that workflow’s draft defaults; Save workflows keeps them and Cancel leaves saved preferences unchanged.

05 / Deckadence → VersaWorks

A Roland handoff checklist

  1. Choose the process.For white ink on reflective stock, start with Roland UV / VersaWorks. Confirm the printer has white ink and a suitable media setup.
  2. Export the saved card masks.Enable Include saved spot-foiling ink layers · PDF and choose Artwork + named spot layers. Deckadence creates the RDG_WHITE separation. Use Preserve tool percentages for soft edges and partial white coverage.
  3. Inspect in Illustrator if needed.Check the RDG_WHITE spot fill and coverage mask using the steps below. If you save another PDF, preserve spot colors, transparency, and the original page dimensions.
  4. Inspect the RIP preview.Verify the white plate, color plate, scale, and registration. Use the supplied mask without an automatic full-white underbase filling its intentional openings.
  5. Print a small proof.Check white opacity, foil visibility, edge alignment, text, and fine details. Try solid coverage first; assess intermediate tints on the actual materials before a full run.

White-data preparation follows Roland’s Illustrator guide ↗. RIP controls depend on the installed printer and VersaWorks version.

The foil vanished, or the whole card is reflective

Check mask inversion and the RIP’s generated-underbase settings. A full white flood can cover intended foil openings; a missing or unrecognized white separation can leave the entire stock exposed.

The white plate is missing or prints as a color

Confirm that the file contains a true spot separation, its name matches the RIP swatch, and export did not convert it to CMYK. The color used to display a spot on screen is not the physical ink assignment.

How do I view the artwork and spot mask separately?

New exports place artwork above the spot layer, with the optional White Inverse Layer on top. In a PDF viewer with layer controls, hide artwork and the white inverse to view the spot mask in its configured display color. To view the original artwork, show artwork and hide both other layers. A black-only mask PDF omits artwork; choose Artwork + named spot layers to include it.

How do I check the spot fill in Illustrator?

Use a newly exported Artwork + named spot layers PDF. In Illustrator, expand its clipping group in Layers and select the filled path, rather than the clipping path or artwork image. Its Fill should be the RDG_WHITE spot swatch. The Transparency panel contains the coverage mask. For ink visibility, choose File → Document Color Mode → CMYK Color, open Window → Separations Preview, and enable Overprint Preview. Toggle RDG_WHITE to inspect the ink separately. A blank Fill for an image or clipping path does not identify the ink used elsewhere in the document.

The white plate looks blocky or speckled

For dots and holes in a mask you want to keep, use Remove small pockets, choose a maximum area, and click Apply pocket cleanup. Start small and increase gradually, or use Custom for one-pixel increments. This keeps larger shapes and current brush edits. Older automatic masks were generated from a small analysis image; to rebuild one, reopen the card with high-quality artwork, choose Balanced cleanup, and click Apply spot foiling to regenerate from the current artwork. Regeneration replaces previous touch-ups, so review and refine before saving; Undo is available in the editor. Save the card and export a new PDF. A bitmap still reveals pixels at extreme zoom. Intermediate ink percentages may also be screened into dots by the RIP; test those percentages on the intended stock.

Edges have a white halo or masks do not line up

Check identical page dimensions, rotation, scale, and placement before adjusting the ink-area shrink setting. Avoid applying the same choke in both the file and the RIP without accounting for their combined effect.
